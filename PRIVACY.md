# SEER Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 5, 2026

SEER ("the app") is provided by the SEER team ("we", "our", "us").

## Information We Collect

SEER is designed to minimize data collection.

- API credentials you enter (such as API keys) are stored on your device using iOS secure storage.
- Chat content and app settings are stored locally on your device.
- Optional diagnostics or telemetry events may be collected to improve reliability and performance.

## How We Use Information

We use information to:

- operate the app and provide AI chat functionality
- save your preferences and conversation state
- diagnose crashes, errors, and performance issues

## Third-Party Services

SEER connects to third-party model and API providers selected or configured by the user.  
When you send prompts, attachments, or messages, that content is transmitted to the configured provider and is subject to that provider’s terms and privacy policy.

## Data Storage and Retention

- Local chat history and settings remain on your device unless you delete the app or clear data.
- We do not sell personal information.
- We do not use your content for advertising.

## Security

We use reasonable technical safeguards, including encrypted network transport (HTTPS/TLS) and secure on-device storage where available.

## Children’s Privacy

SEER is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Your Choices

You can:

- remove or replace API credentials in app settings
- delete conversations from within the app
- uninstall the app to remove local app data

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted at this URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
